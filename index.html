La especialidad

Birria, four ways.

Beef, marinated in an adobo of dried chiles, garlic, cumin, bay and thyme, then simmered low and slow until it gives in to its own consommé. In Mexico it is the dish of weddings and holidays. Here, it is any day we are open.

Nuestra casa

A small house with a big table.

Casa Alejo opened its doors on Emma Avenue in the fall of 2022 with a simple idea: cook the food we love the long way, and welcome everyone who walks in like they have been here before.

Inside you will find nooks and booths, a bar where the staff is happy to talk, and a patio out front for watching downtown go by. We are proud to be part of Springdale, at its events, on its sidewalks and beside our neighbors when they need a hand.

Preguntas

Good to know.

Something we missed? Call us at (479) 365-7222 and we will be happy to help.

Where are you, and where do I park?

We are at 101 W Emma Ave in downtown Springdale, at the corner of Emma and Holcomb. Parking is on the street along Emma Avenue and the surrounding blocks.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 11 AM to 8 PM. Friday and Saturday, 11 AM to 10 PM. We are closed on Sunday. Holiday hours can vary, so call ahead if you are making a special trip.

What exactly is birria?

A celebratory Mexican stew of adobo-marinated meat, slow cooked in a broth of dried chiles, herbs and spices. We make ours with beef, and that broth becomes the consommé you dip everything into.

What does “Don Alejo Style” mean?

It is our signature move: make any dish with ribeye for $5 more. Availability is limited, and it does not apply to fajitas.

Do you have a bar and a patio?

Both. There is a full bar inside and a patio out front on Emma Avenue.

Can I order to go, or bring a big group?

Yes. Order for pickup or delivery on DoorDash, or call us at (479) 365-7222. For larger groups, a call ahead helps us have a table ready for you.

Is there another Casa Alejo?

Yes. Our second home opened in downtown Rogers in 2024, with the same kitchen and the same welcome.
